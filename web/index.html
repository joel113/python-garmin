      Shared across all routes and tracks. Full/partial labels describe completion of the planned route. Travel in either direction and all sports are included. Matches must share at least 250 m, or half the route for routes shorter than 500 m.

      Komoot and Garmin file exchange

      Export a planned route as GPX from your route planner, then upload it here. Download the original GPX to import it as a course in Garmin Connect. Notes are saved and downloaded separately. Direct Komoot account import and Garmin API publishing are not part of this version.
